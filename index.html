How to export your course from Canvas
  1. Open your course in Canvas and go to Settings (bottom of the left-hand navigation).
  2. Click Export Course Content in the right-hand sidebar.
  3. Select Course as the export type, then click Create Export.
  4. Wait for Canvas to prepare the file — this usually takes less than a minute. A download link will appear when it's ready.
  5. Download the .imscc file and upload it below.

The exported file stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server or shared with anyone.
